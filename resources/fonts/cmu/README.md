# CMU Serif

The text face of the OpenSheaf documents on the web (the default pages of an
archive and the woven literate programs): CMU Serif, from the Computer Modern
Unicode fonts by Andrey V. Panov, after the Computer Modern of Donald Knuth,
the face of TeX and of TeXmacs. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
with the Reserved Font Family Name "Computer Modern Unicode fonts"
(`CMU-LICENSE.txt`, which lists the authors).

`cmunrm.woff2` (roman), `cmunti.woff2` (italic), `cmunbx.woff2` (bold) and
`cmunbi.woff2` (bold italic) are the OpenType fonts of version 0.7.0
(https://ctan.org/pkg/cm-unicode), compressed as WOFF2 with fonttools and not
subset or changed otherwise, so that they keep their name. They are served to
every origin; the pages that use them load them from `opensheaf.org`.
