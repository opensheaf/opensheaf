# EB Garamond

The text face of the OpenSheaf pages on the web: EB Garamond, by Georg Duffner
and Octavio Pardo, Copyright 2017 The EB Garamond Project Authors
(https://github.com/octaviopardo/EBGaramond12), under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (`EBGaramond-LICENSE.txt`).

`EBGaramond-Roman.woff2` and `EBGaramond-Italic.woff2` are the variable fonts
(weights 400 to 800) of https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ebgaramond,
subset with `pyftsubset` (fonttools) to Latin, Greek, punctuation and common
mathematical symbols, with every OpenType feature kept, and compressed as
WOFF2. They are served to every origin, and the Explorer, the default pages of
an archive and the woven literate programs use them from here or as copies.
