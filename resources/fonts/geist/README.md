# Geist

The face of the OpenSheaf sites (this site and the Explorer), and of the
apparatus of every OpenSheaf page: Geist, by Vercel with Basement Studio,
Copyright 2024 The Geist Project Authors (https://github.com/vercel/geist-font),
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`Geist-LICENSE.txt`).

`Geist-Roman.woff2` and `Geist-Italic.woff2` are the variable fonts (weights
100 to 900) of https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geist, version
1.800, compressed as WOFF2 with fonttools, unchanged otherwise. They are served
to every origin: the Explorer keeps copies, the default pages of an archive
and the woven literate programs use them from here.
